Eazychat Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Overview

Eazychat ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, handle, store, and safeguard your data when using our web and mobile applications.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: Display name, username, and password hash (encrypted via bcrypt). We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Communications: Real-time messages, audio recordings (voice notes), and photos that you choose to send to other participants.
  • Device & Media Permissions: Microphone and Camera permissions are requested solely when you record voice notes or participate in WebRTC voice/video calls. Media library access is requested only when you choose to share a photo.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Expo push tokens registered to deliver alerts when new messages or calls arrive while the app is in the background.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the collected information exclusively to provide real-time messaging, audio/video calling, profile customization, and push notifications. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to any third-party advertisers.

4. User Safety, Blocking & Content Moderation

Eazychat provides tools for user safety:

  • Block User: You can block any user to immediately prevent direct communications and calls.
  • Report Abuse: You can submit abuse reports regarding unwanted conduct or policy violations. Reports are reviewed by administrators.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete your account at any time directly within the app (Settings → Delete Account). Deletion immediately purges your profile, associated credentials, chats, and sent messages from our servers.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our privacy practices, please contact us at support@eazytap.com.
